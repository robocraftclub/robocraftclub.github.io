Who we are

A special interest group backed by the Industrial Centre

RoboCraft Club is a PolyU special interest group supported by the Industrial Centre. We run project spaces and a fleet of robots — Universal Robots arms, drones, autonomous cars, robotic hands, 3D-printed humanoids and more.

In the workshop

What members are building

Every robot here started as a sketch, a spool of filament and a bag of servos. Members design, print, wire and code them in the club.

Why join

Learn by building, not by exams

Hands-on first

Plug in a robot, run a demo, then hack it. You work with real hardware from day one.

Coding, electronics & AI

Pick up practical skills project by project — firmware, CAD, sensors, machine learning.

Find your crew

Team up with students and staff who also love robots, from beginners to competition veterans.

Everyone's welcome

No entry bar — you don't need to know coding or mechanics. Students and staff from every department can join.
